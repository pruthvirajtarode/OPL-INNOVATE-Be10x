The OPL Prompt Framework

Use this structure for every prompt. Every element matters.

T
Task
What do you want Copilot to do?
C
Context
Who is the audience? What is the goal?
S
Source
Which specific files or data to use?
C
Constraint
What limits? "Only use provided data"
O
Output
Format, length, tone?
✓
Check
What must a human verify?

10 Rescue Lines

When Copilot's first answer isn't right — use these exact phrases.

01
"Make this more concise. Maximum 5 bullet points."
02
"Give me this in a table format."
03
"What assumptions did you make? List them."
04
"Use ONLY the information I provided. Nothing else."
05
"Show me the evidence for each claim."
06
"Rewrite this for a senior banking stakeholder."
07
"Give me three alternative approaches."
08
"What information is missing from this analysis?"
09
"Flag anything that needs human confirmation before sharing."
10
"Do NOT invent dates, numbers, owners, or commitments."

VERIFY
"List every conclusion you just made that is NOT directly supported by the data I provided. Flag each as UNVERIFIED."
EXECUTIVE
"Turn these portfolio findings into a six-slide executive summary. Clearly label: PUBLIC OPL FACTS vs SYNTHETIC ANALYSIS. Include next steps."
EMAIL
"Draft a concise stakeholder update based ONLY on these findings. Tone: professional, banking context. Flag items that need confirmation before sending."
MEETING
"From this meeting transcript, extract: DECISIONS, ACTIONS (owner + date), OPEN QUESTIONS. Use [CONFIRM OWNER] and [CONFIRM DATE] where not stated. DO NOT invent."
