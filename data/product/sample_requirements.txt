BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
Feature: Export to PDF

1. Overview
Users currently can only view reports in the web browser. They need the ability to export these reports to PDF format for offline sharing and compliance archiving.

2. Functional Requirements
- FR1: A prominent "Export to PDF" button must be visible on all report pages.
- FR2: The exported PDF must include the company logo and timestamp in the header.
- FR3: Data tables must maintain formatting and column alignment in the PDF.
- FR4: If the report exceeds 1 page, pagination must be applied automatically (Page X of Y).
- FR5: The system must support downloading reports up to 50 pages within 10 seconds.

3. Non-Functional Requirements
- NFR1: The feature must comply with WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
- NFR2: PDF generation must not block the main application thread for other users.
